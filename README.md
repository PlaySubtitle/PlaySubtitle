**PlaySubtitle** ([playsubtitle.com](https://playsubtitle.com)) shows translated subtitles for the video and audio you play, while it plays.

- On a **Windows 10 or 11 PC**, **Video Player** and **Live Overlay** recognise and translate speech on your own PC. Your audio is not uploaded, there is no API key, and nothing is charged per minute.
- In **Chrome or Edge** on any computer, **PlaySubtitle Online** makes translated subtitles for video files.

## Free and Pro

**Video Player**
- **Free** never expires. It shows the original-language line for the whole video and translates every line in the first fifth of each video, then about 7 in 10. In Web Mode, videos with captions are translated.
- **Pro** translates every line for the whole video, makes subtitles for web videos that have no captions, and adds karaoke-style word highlighting and custom subtitle colours.

**Live Overlay**
- **Free** never expires and shows live subtitles in the language being spoken.
- **Pro** translates them into your language.

**Windows Pro** is a yearly subscription: cancel any time, full refund within 7 days of any payment, one computer per licence (the Bundle: one computer per product). Free does not ask you to sign in. Pro uses a free PlaySubtitle account, confirmed with a one-time code sent by e-mail; there is no password.

**PlaySubtitle Online** is sold separately: Free includes 5 minutes of translated subtitles a day, and Pro is a 7-Day Pass, a Monthly or a Yearly plan.

## Privacy and the internet

**Windows apps (Video Player, Live Overlay)**
- What you watch and hear is processed on your PC and is not uploaded.
- After setup, Local Player and Live Overlay work without a connection.
- The internet is needed once to download the language data (about 3 GB), for Web Mode, and for Pro to activate and check its activation (it works offline for up to 30 days between checks).

**PlaySubtitle Online**
- Your video and its sound stay in your browser.
- The text of the subtitles is sent to PlaySubtitle's servers to be translated and is not kept, so it needs the internet.

## Will it run on my PC? (Windows apps)

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- Recommended: an NVIDIA GeForce graphics card with 6 GB of video memory (GTX 1060 or newer)
- Without one, PlaySubtitle runs on the processor and subtitles arrive noticeably later
- Minimum 8 GB RAM
- 15 GB free disk space

PlaySubtitle Online runs in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones and tablets are not supported yet.

## What PlaySubtitle does not do

- It does not dub; it shows subtitles only.
- It does not translate text shown on the screen (menus, signs, game text).
- It does not export subtitle files.
- The Windows apps run on Windows 10 and 11 only.
- Subtitles are machine translation and can contain mistakes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does PlaySubtitle need an API key?
No. Video Player and Live Overlay recognise and translate speech on your PC. There is no API key and nothing is charged per minute.

### Does it need the internet?
Once, to download the language data (about 3 GB). After setup, Local Player and Live Overlay work without a connection. Web Mode needs the internet, and Pro checks its activation (offline for up to 30 days between checks). PlaySubtitle Online always needs the internet.

### Is my audio uploaded?
Not by the Windows apps: what you watch and hear is processed on your PC. In PlaySubtitle Online, your video and its sound stay in your browser; only the text of the subtitles is sent to be translated, and it is not kept.

### Can it translate live streams, games or videos in a browser?
Yes. Live Overlay shows subtitles over any app from the sound your PC plays. Video Player can also open video sites in its built-in browser (Web Mode).

### How is it different from batch subtitle generators?
Batch tools process the whole file to make a subtitle file before you watch. PlaySubtitle shows subtitles while the video or audio plays, and does not make subtitle files.
